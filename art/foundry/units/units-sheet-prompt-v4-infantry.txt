A sprite candidate sheet on a PURE WHITE background (#FFFFFF). This is a
flat sprite sheet for a 2D strategy game, NOT a scene: no ground, no
horizon, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no grid lines, no borders.
Each sprite is separate and free-standing, surrounded by clean white. No
sprite touches another sprite, and no sprite comes closer than 60 pixels to
any edge of the canvas or closer than 40 pixels to another sprite.

This sheet is INFANTRY SQUADS ONLY. EXACTLY THREE rows, four squads per
row:

- TOP ROW: four squads of THREE riflemen each.
- MIDDLE ROW: four squads of FOUR riflemen each.
- BOTTOM ROW: four squads of FIVE riflemen each.

Each squad is ONE compact sprite: its soldiers stand close together,
shoulders overlapping or nearly touching, forming a single connected
cluster with no wide gaps — a tight fighting group, not a spread line. Each
squad's cluster fills a roughly circular footprint about 300 pixels across.

FIGURES (critical): stocky game-sprite proportions, NOT realistic
proportions — broad shoulders, thick chunky limbs, oversized helmets, big
boots, short legs. Solid, blocky little soldiers that stay readable when
shrunk very small. Each standing figure is roughly 150-180 pixels tall. No
thin spindly rifles floating in space: rifles are held tight against the
body silhouette.

FACING (critical): every rifleman in every squad faces the SAME direction —
due WEST (left) — with his rifle aimed due west. Never mix facings within a
squad; no soldier looks east, toward the viewer, or away from the viewer.

CAMERA (critical): a HIGH angle, viewed from steeply above — helmet tops
and shoulders dominate each figure, with only a small hint of each
soldier's side visible below. NOT a side view, NOT eye-level, NOT
isometric — close to top-down, softened just enough that the figures stay
readable as little soldiers.

READABILITY (critical): these sprites display very small over dark forest
canopy and busy rooftop textures. Every squad needs a BOLD, CONTINUOUS dark
outline around the whole cluster silhouette — clearly heavier than interior
linework — with simplified chunky geometry and strong value contrast.

VARIETY: the four squads within each row must be four DIFFERENT poses and
arrangements — firing line, advancing, kneeling mix, weapon variations —
while keeping the same scale, facing, camera, and style, so any one could
be chosen as THE infantry sprite.

Style: painterly 16-bit military strategy-game sprites matching the
reference image's muted, naturalistic register — soft painted shading
inside bold outlines.

TEAM COLOUR KEY (critical): paint every team-identification area in ONE
single medium cobalt blue (#3B5FC0) — the soldiers' tunics and helmets.
Shading inside these areas must stay the SAME cobalt blue hue, only lighter
or darker — never drifting toward teal, cyan, or purple. Everything else
keeps natural materials: gunmetal grey rifles, khaki webbing, packs, and
trousers, brown boots and wooden stocks, natural skin tones. No insignia of
any kind — no stars, crosses, roundels, flags, or markings.
