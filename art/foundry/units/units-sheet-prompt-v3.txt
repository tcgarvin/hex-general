A sprite candidate sheet on a PURE WHITE background (#FFFFFF). This is a
flat sprite sheet for a 2D strategy game, NOT a scene: no ground, no
horizon, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no grid lines, no borders.
Each unit is a separate free-standing sprite surrounded by clean white. No
sprite touches another sprite, and no sprite comes closer than 60 pixels to
any edge of the canvas or closer than 40 pixels to another sprite.

EXACTLY THREE rows of sprites, five sprites per row:

- TOP ROW: five infantry squads. Each squad is ONE compact sprite of 2-3
  WWII-era riflemen standing close together (a single connected group).
  Every rifleman in every squad faces the SAME direction — due WEST (left)
  — with his rifle aimed due west. Never mix facings within a squad; no
  soldier looks east, toward the viewer, or away from the viewer.
- MIDDLE ROW: five medium tanks of mid-1940s design, each a single tank,
  hull and barrel heading due WEST (left).
- BOTTOM ROW: five towed field guns (artillery pieces) with a split-trail
  carriage and a flat armour shield, barrel pointing due WEST (left).
  Guns 1-3: the gun alone. Guns 4-5: the gun plus two crew figures beside
  it, drawn as one connected sprite, both crewmen also facing west.

HEADING (critical): every unit heads due WEST (left). The tank hull's long
axis runs exactly horizontal across the image, barrel pointing straight
left. The field gun's barrel points straight left. Do NOT angle any unit
toward or away from the viewer — no front-quarter or rear-quarter headings,
nothing pointing diagonally.

CAMERA (critical): a HIGH angle, viewed from steeply above — the TOP
surfaces dominate each sprite: the tank's deck and turret top, the
soldiers' helmet tops and shoulders, the gun's shield top edge and carriage
seen mostly from above. Only a small hint of each unit's south-facing side
is visible below the top surfaces. NOT a side view, NOT isometric — close
to top-down, softened just enough to stay readable.

READABILITY (critical): these sprites will be displayed very small, over
dark forest canopy and busy rooftop textures. Every sprite needs a BOLD,
CONTINUOUS dark outline around its entire silhouette — clearly heavier than
typical interior linework — and simplified, chunky geometry: large clear
shapes, strong value contrast between the sprite and its own outline, no
fine spindly details, no thin protruding parts that vanish when shrunk.

VARIETY: the five sprites in a row must be five DIFFERENT designs — vary
the squad poses and arrangements, the tank silhouettes and stowage, the gun
proportions and carriage details — while keeping the same scale, heading,
camera, and style, so any one could be chosen as THE sprite for that type.

Style: painterly 16-bit military strategy-game sprites matching the
reference images' muted, naturalistic register — soft painted shading
inside the bold outlines, chunky readable silhouettes. All sprites share
one consistent scale: each tank is roughly 300 pixels long, and a standing
soldier's height is about half a tank's length.

TEAM COLOUR KEY (critical): paint every team-identification area in ONE
single medium cobalt blue (#3B5FC0) — the soldiers' tunics and helmets, the
tanks' hull and turret armour panels, and the field guns' carriage, wheel
hubs, and shield. Shading inside these areas must stay the SAME cobalt blue
hue, only lighter or darker — never drifting toward teal, cyan, or purple.
Everything that is NOT a team area keeps natural materials: gunmetal grey
barrels, tracks, and running gear; brown wooden grips and stocks; khaki
webbing, packs, and trousers; natural skin tones. No national insignia of
any kind — no stars, crosses, roundels, flags, or markings.
