A sprite candidate sheet on a PURE WHITE background (#FFFFFF). This is a
flat sprite sheet for a 2D strategy game, NOT a scene: no ground, no
horizon, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no grid lines, no borders.
Each unit is a separate free-standing sprite surrounded by clean white. No
sprite touches another sprite, and no sprite comes closer than 60 pixels to
any edge of the canvas or closer than 40 pixels to another sprite.

EXACTLY THREE rows of sprites, five sprites per row:

- TOP ROW: five infantry squads. Each squad is ONE compact sprite of 2-3
  WWII-era riflemen standing close together (a single connected group),
  rifles in hand.
- MIDDLE ROW: five medium tanks of mid-1940s design, each a single tank.
- BOTTOM ROW: five towed field guns (artillery pieces) with a split-trail
  carriage and a flat armour shield. Guns 1-3: the gun alone. Guns 4-5: the
  gun plus two crew figures standing beside it, drawn as one connected
  sprite.

HEADING (critical): every unit is shown in STRICT SIDE PROFILE heading due
WEST (left) — like classic strategy-game sprites. The tank hull's long axis
runs exactly horizontal across the image, barrel pointing straight left; we
see the tank's side, not its front corner. The field gun's barrel points
straight left, carriage in side profile. The riflemen stand in side profile
facing left. Do NOT angle any unit toward or away from the viewer — no
front-quarter or rear-quarter headings, nothing pointing diagonally.

CAMERA: side profile with only a slight top-down tilt — the camera is
raised just enough that a narrow strip of each top surface (tank deck,
helmet tops, gun shield top edge) is visible above the side. Mostly a side
view. NOT isometric, NOT a 45-degree view, NOT top-down.

VARIETY: the five sprites in a row must be five DIFFERENT designs — vary
the silhouette details, stowage, poses, and small equipment — while keeping
the same scale, the same heading, the same camera, and the same style, so
any one of them could be chosen as THE sprite for that unit type.

Style: painterly 16-bit military strategy-game sprites matching the
reference images' muted, naturalistic register — soft painted shading, a
thin dark outline around each sprite, chunky readable silhouettes that stay
legible when shrunk very small. All sprites share one consistent scale:
each tank is roughly 300 pixels long, and a standing soldier's height is
about half a tank's length.

TEAM COLOUR KEY (critical): paint every team-identification area in ONE
single medium cobalt blue (#3B5FC0) — the soldiers' tunics and helmets, the
tanks' hull and turret armour panels, and the field guns' carriage, wheel
hubs, and shield. Shading inside these areas must stay the SAME cobalt blue
hue, only lighter or darker — never drifting toward teal, cyan, or purple.
Everything that is NOT a team area keeps natural materials: gunmetal grey
barrels, tracks, and running gear; brown wooden grips and stocks; khaki
webbing, packs, and trousers; natural skin tones. No national insignia of
any kind — no stars, crosses, roundels, flags, or markings.
